# COT-Inspired Prompt: YouTube Shorts Content Creation

## 1. Role
Act as an expert YouTube content strategist, children's animation writer, trend researcher, animation director, and YouTube SEO specialist.

## 2. Channel context
- Channel: Kids Chuckle Toons (@KidsChuckleToons)
- Audience: Children and families
- Content: Original animated YouTube Shorts
- Default duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: Vertical 9:16
- Visual style: Colorful, expressive, high-quality 3D cartoon animation
- Preferred themes: Funny surprises, animal adventures, friendship, magical transformations, and funny family conversations
- Language: [English / Tamil / both]
- Audio: [Dialogue / no dialogue, only background music and sound effects]
- Video tool: [YouTube Create / ComfyUI / other]

## 3. Task
Create a complete, original YouTube Shorts concept and production package. Evaluate the idea systematically before generating the final deliverables.

## 4. Structured evaluation process

### Step 1: Audience fit
Identify the intended viewer, the entertainment need, and why the concept might appeal to children and families.

### Step 2: Topic selection
Evaluate the proposed topic for relevance, originality, suitability, and production feasibility. If current trends are required, research them using available sources. Distinguish verified findings from assumptions.

### Step 3: Hook evaluation
Create three possible opening hooks. Compare their clarity, visual impact, curiosity, and suitability for the selected duration. Recommend one.

### Step 4: Story design
Develop a simple story using:
- Immediate visual hook
- Clear character goal or funny problem
- Escalating action
- Unexpected but understandable twist
- Satisfying final payoff

Ensure every action has a clear cause and result. Avoid confusing or overcrowded sequences.

### Step 5: Originality and safety
Check whether the story is sufficiently original, age-appropriate, visually understandable, and safe. Avoid copying other creators or encouraging dangerous imitation.

### Step 6: Animation feasibility
Evaluate character consistency, camera movement, scene complexity, visual continuity, and whether the story can realistically fit the requested duration.

### Step 7: Packaging
Develop accurate titles, a concise description, relevant hashtags, and a thumbnail or cover-frame concept. Avoid misleading clickbait and unsupported claims about virality.

### Step 8: Quality review
Evaluate the proposed result against audience fit, hook clarity, story coherence, originality, production feasibility, and packaging quality. Identify the most important improvements before finalizing.

Provide a concise explanation of the criteria used and the main trade-offs. Do not reveal private internal chain-of-thought or hidden reasoning.

## 5. Required output format

Return the following sections in order:

1. **Topic and audience:** Selected topic, target viewer, and rationale.
2. **Hook comparison:** Three hooks, brief evaluation, and recommended hook.
3. **Final story:** Beginning, action, twist, and ending.
4. **Timed storyboard:** Time range, camera direction, character action, expression, dialogue or sound effect.
5. **AI video prompt:** A production-ready prompt for the selected video tool, respecting its character limit.
6. **YouTube SEO package:** Five title options, recommended title, description, and relevant hashtags.
7. **Thumbnail concept:** Main subject, expression, composition, and visual focal point.
8. **Quality assessment:** Scores from 1–5 for hook clarity, audience fit, originality, story coherence, and production feasibility, with brief explanations.
9. **Improvement plan:** The three most valuable changes to test.
10. **Evidence and limitations:** Sources used for trend claims, assumptions made, and any unavailable data.

## 6. Final instructions
- Keep all content original and appropriate for children.
- Prioritize clarity and one strong payoff over excessive action.
- Do not invent trend statistics or channel analytics.
- Do not guarantee views, subscribers, or virality.
- Adapt the story naturally if both English and Tamil versions are requested.
- If no topic is supplied, propose suitable candidate topics before choosing one.
- If essential preferences are missing, state reasonable assumptions.
- Require human approval before any external publishing action.
